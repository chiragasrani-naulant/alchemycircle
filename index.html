Our

#Story

Good therapy shouldn’t depend on your city or your savings.

Finding a therapist in India felt like guesswork, so we built one place for vetted professionals and warm community spaces. Asking for help should take minutes, not months.

Questions people ask

Are sessions confidential?

Yes. What you share stays between you and your therapist, except where the law requires otherwise or someone is in immediate danger.

How much does a session cost?

Prices are on each profile. Intro calls are free, and some therapists offer sliding-scale slots.

Can I switch therapists?

Anytime. It often takes a couple of tries to find the right fit.

Is this for emergencies?

No. In a crisis, call Tele-MANAS at 14416 (free, 24×7) or your local emergency number.
